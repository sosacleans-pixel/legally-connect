Case intake form

Thank you for talking with us. This form collects the details an independent attorney will want to know about your accident. Fill in what you know. It is fine to leave blanks.

Before you start

  • It takes about 15 to 25 minutes. Fields marked *required are required. Everything else is optional.
  • If you are filling this out for someone else, answer for the person who was hurt.
  • Helpful to have nearby: insurance cards, any police report number, and the names of doctors or clinics you have visited.
  • This form never asks for a Social Security number. Please do not type one anywhere on this form.
  • Legal documents, such as an attorney agreement or medical records releases, are not part of this form. If you decide to hire an attorney, those are handled directly with the attorney.
